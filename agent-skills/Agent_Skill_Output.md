1. Title
Neon Orbit Snap

2. Core Mechanic
A glowing outer ring continuously shrinks toward the center of the screen. In the center is a fixed, static "target ring." The player must tap or click at the exact millisecond the shrinking outer ring perfectly overlaps the static target ring.

3. Progression
Upon a successful hit, the shrinking ring immediately resets to the outer edge and collapses faster. With every 5 successful hits, the static target ring becomes thinner, requiring greater precision. A "Game Over" triggers if the player taps too early, or if the shrinking ring completely passes through the target ring without the player tapping.

4. UI Elements
Current Score (Large, slightly transparent text positioned in the background center)

High Score (Small text in the top-right corner)

Game Over text and a Restart Button that appear when the player misses
